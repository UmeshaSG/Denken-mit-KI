<p class="tagline">Denken mit KI: German for "thinking with AI".</p>

This is where I write down what I do with AI. The experiments that work. The ones that don't. And the ideas that come out of both.

## What you'll find here

- **Experiments.** Things I try with AI tools, and what actually happened. Failures included.
- **Thinking.** Frameworks and methods for thinking with AI without handing over my judgment.
- **Doing.** How I use AI in real work, and where it earns its place.
- **The landscape.** My take on new models, tools and ideas, and what they mean for the rest of us.

## How I write

Short and plain. I say what I tried, what I saw, and what I think. If I'm not sure, I say so.

> Use it. Break it. Tell me where it fails.

## Talk to me

Every post has a comment section at the bottom. Sign in with GitHub and tell me where I'm wrong, or what you'd try next.
